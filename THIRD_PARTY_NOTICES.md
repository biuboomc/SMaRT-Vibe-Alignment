and `overlays/verl/` preserves focused replacement files and CPU tests.

notices are retained.

documentation outside these directories. The Reader data bundle contains generated
derivatives of the benchmarks listed in `data/reader_v3/README.md`; it is not
covered by the software licenses. No raw benchmark dumps or model weights are
redistributed.
